I've been looking for a personal dashboard to help keep me organised and motivated. I wanted something simple, offline, easy to access, and nice to look at, but I couldn't quite find what I was looking for, so I decided to make my own.

![dashboard](/images/dashboard.png)

Since I wanted something offline, I initially thought of making a desktop app, but I thought that would be too excessive for a simple dashboard. The only other offline interface I could think of was the terminal, so I made a terminal dashboard, using python and the Rich library. 

It was a fun process! I had highly underestimated the visual capabilities of the terminal. Thankfully, the Rich documentation was also clear and easy to follow.
